A conceptual exterior twin

            
This is an independent, real-time 3D study of the exterior of Iris Residences at Anandtara, Mundhwa, Pune. Every visible element — massing, facade rhythm, open crown, landscape, gate — is geometry generated in the browser from a visual reading of published project imagery. It is not a photograph panorama, a video, or an official developer model.

            
No architect-issued model, drawing set or survey was available for this exercise.

          

          

            
What is verified, and what is inferred

            
The elongated three-wing white slab, deep facade channels, horizontal slab bands, glazed balconies, bronze screens, broad end blades, sculptural end visors, podium and open rooftop crown are all visible in the supplied references. Their approximate dimensions and internal rhythm are inferred visually.

            
Sizes are estimates: approximately 64 m long, 20 m deep, 14 residential levels, with the terrace at about 46.8 m and the open crown top near 54.7 m. The parapet, crown and restrained roof forms follow what is visible in the published aerial views; no rooftop amenity is claimed. The driveway, drop-off loop, play garden, court and planting are a conceptual landscape study, not a surveyed site plan.

            
The new IRIS Inside view includes an illustrative sample apartment and room selector to demonstrate interaction only. No approved interior model or verified apartment floor plan was supplied. Do not use the exterior or interior study for construction, approvals, sales, wayfinding, RERA disclosures or any representation of the real property.

          

          

            
Controls

            

              
Orbit
Drag to rotate through a full 360°, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan.

              
Free-look
Drag to look. W A S D or arrow keys move; Q / E (or ▲ / ▼) change height; Shift accelerates. A mobile joystick is provided.

              
Pointer lock
Optional in free-look. Click “Lock cursor” for continuous mouse-look; Escape releases it.

              
Views
Use the numbered camera views, or press 1–8 for presets and 9 for Home. O returns to orbit, F enters free-look, I replays the intro.

            

          

          

            
Lighting & motion

            
The sunset is a procedural sky with a matching image-based lighting capture. The intro can be skipped instantly; any canvas interaction also takes control. If your system requests reduced motion, the opening becomes a still title card and the camera does not move automatically.
